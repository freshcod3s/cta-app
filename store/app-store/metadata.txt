DESCRIPTION (max 4000):
Congress Trade Alerts shows the stock transactions that members of the US House and Senate disclose under the STOCK Act. Everything in it comes from official public records -- no opinions, no ads, no account.

FEATURES
- A single feed of congressional stock trades from both chambers
- Push alerts for specific members and tickers, with an optional minimum trade-size filter
- Trade detail with filing date, transaction type, amount range, and -- on newly filed trades -- a link to the original document on the official .gov source
- A Conflicts view that ranks disclosures by documented late filings and trade size, with committee-overlap chips as a labeled secondary signal
- Related trades for the same ticker or by the same member
- Daily Dive with sector analysis, bipartisan trading patterns, and newly filing members
- Member profiles with full trading history, committee seats, party, state, and chamber
- Ticker pages showing all congressional trades for any stock
- Committee pages with rosters, subcommittees, hearings, markups, and votes
- Leaderboard ranking members two ways -- most disclosures, and longest average filing lag
- Watchlist saved on your device -- shared with our server only as anonymous alert preferences if you enable push
- Full methodology with data sources, update frequency, lag stats, and calculation details

DATA SOURCES
All data derives from public STOCK Act filings published by the US House Office of the Clerk and the US Senate Office of Public Records electronic financial disclosure system. Trades appear in the feed once a filing is at least 24 hours old.

WHO IT'S FOR
- Journalists tracking congressional compliance and conflicts of interest
- Civic-minded citizens watching their representatives' financial activity
- Researchers studying public financial-disclosure data

PRIVACY
No account required. No tracking. No ads. No third-party analytics. If you enable notifications, the only data stored is an anonymous push token plus your alert preferences -- turn push off in Settings to delete both. Source code viewable at github.com/freshcod3s/cta-app.

Congress Trade Alerts is an informational civic-transparency tool. It does not provide investment advice, does not recommend buying or selling any security, and does not connect to brokerages or enable trading. Not affiliated with the US government. Disclosures may be delayed or amended -- verify at disclosures-clerk.house.gov and efdsearch.senate.gov. Free. No ads. No tracking.

APP_REVIEW_NOTES:
Congress Trade Alerts is an informational civic-transparency tool built on public STOCK Act disclosure data: the periodic transaction reports that members of the US House and Senate are required by law to file. All data comes from official government sources -- the US House Office of the Clerk financial disclosure portal and the US Senate Office of Public Records electronic financial disclosure system.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO (re guidelines 3.1.1 and 3.2.1):
- No trading of any kind. The app cannot place, route, or facilitate any order or transaction.
- No brokerage integration and no links to any brokerage, exchange, or trading service.
- No investment advice or recommendations. The app reports what elected officials have publicly disclosed; a prominent not-investment-advice disclaimer appears on the About and Methodology screens.
- No handling of money, payments, deposits, or financial accounts of any kind.
- The word "Trade" in the name refers to the disclosed trades of members of Congress that the app reports on, not to any trading functionality.

ACCOUNTS AND SIGN-IN:
- The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no registration anywhere. Sign-in is not required for review and no demo account exists or is needed: every feature is reachable on first launch.

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS:
- Push is strictly opt-in via a toggle in Settings (off by default). Enabling it registers an anonymous push token plus alert preferences with our server; turning it off deletes both from the server. The push token and the followed members and tickers are the only data stored -- not the user's name, email, or anything that identifies them personally. No analytics SDK, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

PURCHASES (re guideline 3.1.3(f) -- free stand-alone app):
- There is no purchase surface anywhere in the app: no in-app purchases, no external purchase links, no checkout, and no pricing displayed in the app.
- A paid tier exists only on the developer website for the companion web product. The app does not link to that checkout and does not promote it. In this build no screen the app can display -- the FAQ included -- mentions that tier, its price, or the web product's pricing. The FAQ's only cost-related answer, "Does this app cost anything?", speaks solely to the app and answers "No": every feature in this app is free, with no in-app purchases, no subscription, no ads, and no tracking. For completeness: a Subscription card that would link out to the web checkout exists in the source and is compiled out by the build-time constant SHOW_UPGRADE_CTA=false (lib/flags.ts:39), so its strings may appear in a binary string dump while being unreachable at runtime; restoring it would be a separate submission.

METADATA ACCURACY (re guideline 2.3):
- The description and screenshots reflect the shipped build. Price-performance comparisons that exist on the web product are not present in this build and are not claimed in the listing.

Developer: Freshcod3s LLC. Website: https://congresstradealerts.com (privacy policy at /privacy, terms at /terms, data deletion at /delete).
