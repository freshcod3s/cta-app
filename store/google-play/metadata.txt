Congress Trade Alerts is an independent app. It does not represent, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, any government entity.

Explore stock transactions disclosed by members of the US House and Senate, follow members or tickers, and receive alerts about newly collected filings. This is a civic public-records tool, not an investment service.

HOW TO READ THE DATA
Our pipeline collects and normalizes filings. Open an original disclosure from trade details where a source link is available. Methodology in the app explains each source and its limits.

Committee overlap flags compare our own sector classifications with committee jurisdictions. These are app-generated indicators, not government findings or evidence of wrongdoing. Amounts are disclosed as ranges; aggregate figures are estimates.

Our late flag checks for more than 45 days between the recorded trade and disclosure dates. It is not an official violation determination. Reporting rules generally use the earlier of 30 days after notification or 45 days after the transaction; see the official guidance above.

House checks are scheduled about every 30 minutes and Senate checks about every six hours; availability and collection limits can delay or omit records and alerts. The main feed shows filings at least 24 hours old. Alerts and other views may show a record earlier. Alerts do not report trades as they execute.

PRIVACY
No account, ads, or analytics SDKs. Push notifications use a device push token and your selected preferences. Disable notifications in Settings to remove that registration. No brokerage connection, trade execution, or investment advice.
