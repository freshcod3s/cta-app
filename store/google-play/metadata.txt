APP_NAME (max 30): Congress Stock Trade Alerts
SHORT_DESCRIPTION (max 80): Track House and Senate stock trades with alerts on new STOCK Act disclosures.

FULL_DESCRIPTION (max 4000):
Congress Trade Alerts tracks the stock transactions that members of the US House and Senate disclose under the STOCK Act. Get trade alerts when new filings appear, search and filter by member or company, and open the original government disclosure behind a newly filed trade.

Built as a Congress stock tracker for journalists, researchers, and civic-engaged citizens who want politician stock trades in one legible place, with the primary source one tap away on new filings.

What you get
- A single feed of congressional stock trades from both chambers
- Push alerts when a member you follow files a new disclosure
- Follow any member or company ticker to shape the alerts you receive
- A trade constellation on every member profile: disclosed trades sized by amount, grouped around committee assignments
- Committee overlap flags when a traded company intersects the filer's committee jurisdiction -- a transparency signal, not a finding of wrongdoing
- Late-filing flags when a disclosure lands past the 45-day STOCK Act deadline
- One tap from a newly filed trade to its original document on the official .gov source
- A plain-language methodology explaining how STOCK Act filings become the feed you see

Data sources
The app tracks the disclosures members file with:
- US House Clerk Periodic Transaction Reports (PTR): https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov
- US Senate Office of Public Records (Senate EFD): https://efdsearch.senate.gov/search/
- House filings checked every 30 minutes; Senate every six hours

Privacy
- No account required -- no name, email, or password, ever
- No analytics, no ads, no third-party tracking SDKs
- To deliver the alerts you ask for, the app stores an anonymous device push token and the members and tickers you choose to follow -- not your name, email, or anything that identifies you personally
- Turn off notifications in the app to delete everything tied to your device, anytime
- Open source: https://github.com/freshcod3s/cta-app

Congress Trade Alerts is an informational public-records tool. It does not execute trades, connect to brokerage accounts, or provide investment advice.

This app is not affiliated with the United States government or with any member of Congress. All data comes from public records that members of Congress are required by law to file under the Stop Trading on Congressional Knowledge Act of 2012.

Free. No ads. No account required. Open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.
